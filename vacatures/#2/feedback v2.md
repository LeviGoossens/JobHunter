# Recruiter feedback v2: motivation letter

**Letter score: 7.5/10**

The revised letter is clear, honest and relevant to TSG's vacancy. The testing and migration examples are stronger than before, but the latest edits removed some important context and technology details. Restoring those would make the letter more convincing.

**Recruiter decision:** I would consider an introductory conversation if TSG is open to a junior developer. If the team needs someone who can immediately work independently at medior level, the experience gap may still lead them to prioritize other candidates. The vacancy does, however, emphasize technical independence and willingness to take responsibility alongside years of experience.

## What works well

- **Relevant example:** The migration, data-import tool, user interviews and tests show a range of practical development work.
- **Clearer outcome:** The explanation of the multi-tenant architecture helps the reader understand why the work mattered.
- **Testing:** The two tests for possible data leaks address an explicit requirement in the vacancy. The wording appropriately avoids suggesting that tests guarantee security.
- **Honest positioning:** The junior paragraph acknowledges the experience gap without dwelling on it.
- **Better project chronology:** The Webshop paragraph distinguishes between the group project and your current work.

## Most important improvements

1. **Restore the context for the migration.** The paragraph now begins with “Ik hielp een bestaande .NET-applicatie migreren” without naming the Sint-Vincentius app, PXL smart-ICT, your internship or your studies. A recruiter cannot easily tell where the work took place. For example, begin with:

   > Tijdens mijn graduaat Programmeren aan Hogeschool PXL en mijn stage bij PXL smart-ICT werkte ik aan de Sint-Vincentius-app voor echte gebruikers.

   Follow this with your existing description of the migration, import tool, interviews and tests.

2. **Bring back the most relevant technologies.** The revised Webshop paragraph no longer mentions Blazor, Entity Framework, PostgreSQL or Docker. Blazor in particular is important because TSG specifically asks for it. If accurate, add a sentence such as:

   > Voor dit project gebruikte ik ASP.NET, Blazor, Entity Framework, PostgreSQL en Docker.

   You could also mention the Sint-Vincentius technology stack, but prioritize the details most relevant to the vacancy rather than listing everything.

3. **Identify the type of tests if accurate.** TSG explicitly asks for unit-testing experience. If these were unit tests, say “twee unit tests”; if they were integration tests, call them that instead. Do not label them as unit tests unless they actually were.

4. **Consider trimming repetition.** Maintenance, documentation, code reviews and learning still appear in several paragraphs. Keep the strongest examples and use the space for the missing project context and technology stack.

## Bottom line

The revised letter is more polished and gives better evidence of testing and impact. Restore the internship/project context and the Blazor stack before sending it; those details help a recruiter connect your experience directly to the role.
